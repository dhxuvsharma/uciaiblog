The {{ site_name }} at the University of California, Irvine brings together students, researchers and faculty working on artificial intelligence. This blog is our shared notebook: a place to post papers we've written or loved, keep up with news from across the field, and celebrate what's happening in the lab.

How to contribute

  1. Create an account.
  2. Click Write and pick a category: Paper, AI News, Lab Update, Event or Tutorial.
  3. Add a link to the source and a few sentences in your own words.

Community guidelines

  • Be accurate, and link to primary sources.
  • Credit authors when you summarise their work.
  • Be respectful in comments. Editors may hide posts or comments that break these rules.
